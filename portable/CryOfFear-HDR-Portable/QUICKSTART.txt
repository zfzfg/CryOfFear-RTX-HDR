QUICK INSTALLATION:
-------------------
Method 1 (GUI Configurator):
  1. Double-click "CryOfFearHDRConfig.exe"
  2. Choose resolution (e.g. 2560x1440), FOV, and latency options.
  3. Click Install. Confirm UAC for the driver profile.
  4. If Steam is closed, launch options are written for you. If Steam is
     running, paste the copied line, or close Steam and click Install again:
     -fullscreen -w 2560 -h 1440 -noforcemparms -noforcemaccel -noforcemspd
  5. Turn Windows HDR ON. Auto HDR is already off for cof.exe only.
  6. Optional: "HDR indicator" shows an on-screen marker while RTX HDR runs.

Method 2 (1-Click Batch):
  1. Double-click "INSTALL.bat"
  2. Confirm the UAC prompt (NVIDIA profile import only).
  3. Paste the printed Steam launch options, then launch Cry of Fear.

VERIFICATION:
-------------
Double-click "DIAGNOSE.bat" to verify Windows HDR, Auto HDR override,
GPU driver settings, and game files.

UNINSTALLATION:
---------------
Double-click "UNINSTALL.bat" to restore stock settings.
Saves are never rolled back.
